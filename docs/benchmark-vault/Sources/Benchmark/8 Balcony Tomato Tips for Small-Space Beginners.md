# 8 Balcony Tomato Tips for Small-Space Beginners

This note is a synthetic benchmark source for OMD Home. It contains exactly
eight explicit beginner tips.

## Numbered tips

1. Give each tomato plant at least 6 hours of direct sun every day.
2. Use a container of at least 18 litres with visible drainage holes.
3. Water deeply, then wait until the top 2 cm of soil is dry before watering again.
4. Start weekly tomato feed only after the first flowers open.
5. Tie the main stem to a support before the first fruit cluster gets heavy.
6. Remove leaves that touch the soil to reduce splash-borne disease.
7. Harvest when the fruit colour is even from shoulder to tip, not at the first blush.
8. Keep one notebook line per plant with the date, watering, flowers, and harvest.

## Why these tips matter

- Sunlight prevents weak, leggy growth.
- A larger pot gives roots and moisture more stability.
- Deep watering builds stronger roots than frequent surface sips.
- Early support prevents stem damage later.
